# ⚖️ Compliance Notes

This repository does not claim certification or legal compliance with a particular privacy, security or accessibility regime.

The current product is a local browser game without an account database or remote match service. That reduces the application's data collection surface but does not automatically establish compliance. Hosting providers, dependency licenses, public distribution and future telemetry may introduce separate obligations.

Before adding personal data, payments, tracking, accounts or online play, identify applicable jurisdictions, document collection/retention/deletion, review accessibility, review licenses and obtain qualified advice where needed.
