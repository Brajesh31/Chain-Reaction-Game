# 👥 Player Stories

## New match
- As a host, I want to choose the number of players so a match fits the group.
- As a host, I want to choose board dimensions to vary match length.
- As a new player, I want concise rules before play.

## During a match
- As the active player, I want legal cells to accept a move.
- As a player, I want a clear turn indicator.
- As a player, I want cascades to settle before another turn.
- As a player, I want ownership to be visually distinct.
- As a player, I want invalid moves rejected without state mutation.

## Match completion
- As a player, I want an explicit winner/terminal state.
- As a host, I want to restart with a clean board.

## Accessibility
- As a keyboard user, I want to navigate and activate cells without a pointer.
- As a player with color-vision limitations, I want player identity conveyed by more than color.
- As a motion-sensitive player, I want reduced-motion behavior.

## Evidence
Every story should link to an automated test or manual verification. Online play, accounts and saved matches are future scope.
