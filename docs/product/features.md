# ✨ Feature Guide

## Gameplay
- **Local setup:** choose two, three or four players and a supported grid size.
- **Legal moves:** target an empty cell or a cell owned by the active player.
- **Critical mass:** count in-bounds orthogonal neighbors, producing thresholds of two, three or four on a rectangular board.
- **Chain reaction:** unstable cells burst and distribute one orb to each orthogonal neighbor.
- **Capture:** affected neighbors become owned by the player who initiated the chain.
- **Turn progression:** the game protects opening turns and skips eliminated players.

## Interface
- Title and setup experience.
- Light/dark theme.
- Orb and explosion presentation.
- Active player and winner status.
- Board maximize and optional performance controls.
- Semantic grid with keyboard focus movement.
- Reduced-motion stylesheet support.

Accounts, online rooms, AI, saved matches and rankings are not current features. Consult the [feature inventory](feature-inventory.md) for implementation status and remaining verification.
