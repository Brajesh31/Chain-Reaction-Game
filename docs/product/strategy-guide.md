# ♟️ Strategy Guide

## Read the board
A cell is one orb below critical mass when its count is exactly one less than its number of orthogonal neighbors. It can burst on the next orb placed into it.

## Build connected threats
Connected near-critical cells can create cascade opportunities. A critical cell may also be vulnerable to an opponent's adjacent burst.

## Use edges carefully
Corners and edges have fewer neighbors and lower thresholds. Their behavior is easier to predict, but they can also be easier to trigger.

## Plan captures
A burst changes adjacent ownership to the initiating player. Consider unstable cells created by the move, not only the immediate orb count.

This guide explains general strategy, not guaranteed winning moves. Position, turn order and implemented rules determine outcomes.
