# 👤 Player Personas

These are design personas for planning, not research-backed demographic claims.

## The casual host
Wants to start a match quickly, needs obvious setup choices and clear active-player feedback, and values a simple reset.

## The tactical player
Wants to predict critical cells, bursts and captures. Needs exact rule feedback, consistent turn order and replayable scenarios.

## The accessibility-first player
May rely on keyboard, screen reader, zoom or reduced motion. Needs semantic cell actions, non-color player identity and announced status changes.

## The maintainer
Needs reproducible board fixtures, separation between rules and UI, and repeatable install/test/build commands.

Validate assumptions with playtests rather than presenting these personas as user research.
